## Context

Meta AI for Business is Meta's AI assistant for advertisers. It's an agent that helps advertisers diagnose campaign issues, optimize performance, and create new campaigns through natural conversation.

I led product direction and core agent-system design for what became a 100+ person initiative spanning four Meta organizations.

## What I built

I built the post-training and evaluation loop end to end: SFT, DPO and GRPO, human and LLM-judge evals, and launch gates.

## Outcome

The work grew from a founding team into a cross-organization initiative.

## What I learned

In the AI era, human judgment matters more, not less.
