In 2026 I founded an AI-native marketing startup. As founder, I owned engineering and go-to-market.

## What I built

I built the platform from zero. It had two modes. In analysis mode the agent could only read. In execution mode it showed a plan and waited for a person's approval before changing anything in an ad account.

## Outcome

The startup won multiple paid client engagements.
