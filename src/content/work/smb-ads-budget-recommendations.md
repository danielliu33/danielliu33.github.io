## Context

Small businesses advertise on Meta without the support that large brands pay for, often with no agency and no dedicated marketer. I worked on ad delivery optimization, recommendation systems, and budget automation for those advertisers.

I came to the team from Instagram ads ranking. There I'd built computer-vision super-resolution for ad images and video, aimed at helping small businesses get better creative without expensive production.

## The problem

Every advertiser makes the same few decisions: how much to spend, what to say, and who to reach. A large brand pays experts to help make them. A small business usually makes them alone.

Each system I worked on put a piece of that expertise into the product, so the advertiser didn't have to hire it.

## What came out of it

- Meta's first ML-powered personalized budget recommendation system for SMB advertisers.
- Feed ranking for small-business surfaces, built end to end.
- A U.S. patent application from the same problem space, since published. It covers using large language models to help small businesses create ads, including copy, budget, and audience.

I was tech lead for a workstream of about 10 engineers and 20+ cross-functional partners.

## What I learned

Small advertisers need expert-level judgment that they usually can't afford. Budget recommendations, better creative, and automated ad creation each supplied one piece of it.

A recommendation is one answer to one decision. Agents were my next way of working on the same problem: an assistant that could help across diagnosis, optimization, and campaign creation instead of one decision at a time.
